  <h4 class="section-label">Overview</h4>
  <h2>Redesigned the mentor dashboard to increase user retention and create more intuitive user flow</h2>
  <p>EchoKids is a music education company based in New York City which connects collegiate musicians as mentors to children aged 0-18 years for private and group lessons.</p>
  <p>I was tasked with redesigning their web-app pages to increase user retention and create more intuitive user flow, which is now implemented on all their mobile and desktop platforms.</p>
  <p>This case study will focus on the process of redesigning the mentor dashboard.</p>
  <p><strong>Impact: Increased retention by 200% for EchoKids and a 47% more effective user flow.</strong></p>
