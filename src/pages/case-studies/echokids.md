<section id="overview" class="cs-section">
  <h4 class="section-label">Overview</h4>
  <h2>Redesigning the mentor dashboard to drive retention and streamline user flow</h2>
  <p>
    EchoKids is a music education company based in New York City which connects collegiate musicians as mentors to children aged 0–18 years for private and group lessons.
  </p>
  <p>
    I was tasked with redesigning their web-app pages to increase user retention and create a more intuitive user flow, which is now implemented across all their mobile and desktop platforms. This case study focuses on the end-to-end process of redesigning the mentor dashboard.
  </p>

  <div class="callout">
    <h3>Impact: Increased retention by 200% for EchoKids and created a 47% more effective user flow.</h3>
  </div>

  <div class="grid-2">
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>The Solution</h3>
      <p>
        We redesigned the mentors’ web-app dashboard to comprise a more intuitive user flow and distinctive hierarchies. We rebuilt the company’s brand book with new colors and fonts, establishing brand consistency across both the marketing site and the web-app.
      </p>
      <p>
        We modernized the UI to create a visually engaging, compartmentalized experience that makes finding critical lesson details effortless.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>The Approach</h3>
      <p>
        We worked closely with executive stakeholders, fellow interns, and active mentors to diagnose friction points and pinpoint high-frequency features to prioritize on the dashboard.
      </p>
      <p>
        I utilized this redesign as an opportunity to modernize the visual language, eliminate extraneous cognitive load, and establish a foundational design system for EchoKids.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>

<!-- 3. RESEARCH INSIGHTS & STRATEGY -->
<section class="cs-section">
  <h4 class="section-label">Research Insights</h4>
  <h2>Diagnosing friction in the legacy dashboard and setting strategy</h2>

  <div class="grid-3">
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>What Was Broken</h3>
      <p>
        The legacy dashboard had no clear hierarchy or compartmentalization, making it difficult for users to navigate information. Mentors use this screen constantly and expect quick, low-cognitive-load access to upcoming lessons and earnings. Executive stakeholders also needed a distinct brand identity to stand out against competitors.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>How to Fix It</h3>
      <p>
        Redesign the dashboard with modern UI principles, reducing cognitive fatigue and effort needed to locate essentials. Surface pertinent lesson details upfront while establishing modular containers.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Why It Matters</h3>
      <p>
        These design focuses directly boost weekly engagement, raise platform confidence, and increase user retention. Mentors spend less time searching for what they need and more time teaching.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="full-width-media">
    <img
      src="/images/echokids/MacBook_Pro_14__-_1.png"
      alt="Previous EchoKids Mentor Dashboard Design"
      loading="lazy"
    />
  </div>

  <h3>Final Design Strategy</h3>
  <div class="grid-3">
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Modern UI</h3>
      <p>
        Updating the UI to be efficient and visually pleasing incentivizes mentors to use the platform more often, reducing cognitive load and improving user retention.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Intuitive Organization</h3>
      <p>
        Clear organization and hierarchy are vital to make the user flow effective. The biggest opportunities lay in the menu structure and compartmentalizing core features.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Consistency</h3>
      <p>
        Establishing a cohesive brand book and design system sets EchoKids apart from competitors, eliminates visual clutter with a disciplined color palette, and builds user trust.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="section-divider"></div>
</section>

<!-- 4. MAJOR STRUCTURE CHANGES -->
<section class="cs-section">
  <h4 class="section-label">Structure & Architecture</h4>
  <h2>Transforming navigation and introducing modular compartments</h2>

  <div class="full-width-media">
    <img
      src="/images/echokids/Frame 1086 (1).png"
      alt="Major Structure Changes Overview"
      loading="lazy"
    />
  </div>

  <div class="grid-3">
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Static Sidebar Navigation</h3>
      <p>
        Users reported significant difficulty finding the menu on the web-app. We replaced the hidden hamburger menu with a persistent static sidebar—placing the most-used navigation items front and center to reduce frustration and increase satisfaction.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Structural Compartments</h3>
      <p>
        We introduced modular compartments into the layout. Sectioning information into distinct cards provides full visibility while allowing users to streamline their workflow by quickly scanning upcoming lessons.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Design & Color Hierarchy</h3>
      <p>
        We restricted the palette to avoid distraction and establish clear action hierarchies:
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li><code>#ED8233 Orange</code>: Primary action button in the menu</li>
        <li><code>#28576E Dark Blue</code>: Secondary utility buttons</li>
        <li><code>#FFFFFF White</code>: Content compartments with subtle shadow</li>
        <li><code>#EAF5FB Light Blue</code>: Supporting background</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <h3>Closer Look at the Sectioning of Information</h3>
  <div class="full-width-media">
    <img
      src="/images/echokids/Screen Shot 2022-09-03 at 2.51.17 AM.png"
      alt="Closer Look at Sectioning Information"
      loading="lazy"
    />
  </div>

  <div class="callout">
    <h3>Adopting a compartment design reduces session time and streamlines the mentor’s daily experience.</h3>
  </div>

  <div class="grid-3">
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Adding Headers</h3>
      <p>
        User research showed users struggled to see where each section started and ended. Adding explicit headings signals distinct operational zones, making the dashboard far more scannable.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Information Organization</h3>
      <p>
        Student name and lesson date were moved to the top center for rapid scanning. We kept instrument details on the side and introduced a requested “quick view previous lesson” button so mentors can easily review past notes.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Modernized Visuals</h3>
      <p>
        Reduced color distraction with clean white cards and soft drop shadows. Input text fields were updated with a minimalist aesthetic, and button states were unified to prevent confusion.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>

</section>

<section class="cs-section">
  <h4 class="section-label">Wireframe Synthesis</h4>
  <h2>Synthesizing the best qualities into the final wireframe</h2>

  <div class="grid-3">
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>What</h3>
      <p>
        This final frame takes the best qualities from previous iterations and combines them into a unified experience while rectifying what each predecessor lacked.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>How</h3>
      <p>
        We committed to the persistent static sidebar menu and compensated for tighter horizontal space by adding modular bottom sections and quick links for secondary actions like earning breakdowns.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Why</h3>
      <p>
        The static sidebar navigation maximizes screen efficiency by emphasizing the most frequently used features. It eliminates wasted top whitespace, surfaces high-value tools, and reduces session friction.
      </p>

<!-- 9. REFLECTION SECTION -->
<section id="reflection" class="cs-section">
  <h4 class="section-label">Reflection</h4>
  <h2>Key takeaways & lessons learned</h2>
  <p>
    This was an incredible opportunity where I learned how to cross-functionally communicate across engineering and business leadership to design directly for real-world user needs.
  </p>

  <div class="grid-3">
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>1. Shipped Impact</h3>
      <p>
        This was my first project shipped to production and pushed to active users. The feedback and compliments we received reinforced that the work I do as a product designer has direct impact on people’s daily emotions and productivity. Seeing numerical results—increasing user retention by 200% and creating a 47% more effective user flow—was deeply fulfilling.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>2. Stakeholder Input & Adaptability</h3>
      <p>
        I had to design around technical restrictions and third-party plugin boundaries highlighted by executive leadership. This exposed me to designing within real business constraints and learning how to incorporate them gracefully into user-centric designs without sacrificing quality.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>3. Time Management</h3>
      <p>
        Balancing a high-cadence internship with three concurrent academic courses required strict time management and clear prioritization. Weekly intern critique meetings and specific KPI benchmarks kept our team aligned and enabled me to maintain a harmonious dynamic between school and work.
      </p>
    </div>
