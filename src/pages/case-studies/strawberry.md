  <h2>Leading design at a dual-sided coaching platform scaling from 0 to $30M+ ARR</h2>

I would also take on projects as a PM, coder, and marketer.
  </p>

<section id="operating-model" class="cs-section">
  <h4 class="section-label">Founding Designer Role</h4>
  <h2>Operating at startup velocity: owning product, systems, and code</h2>
  <p>
    In an early-stage startup with only 1 designer, design cannot become a bottleneck. I established a lean operating rhythm designed for speed without sacrificing craft:
  </p>

  <div class="grid-3">
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>0→1 Design System</h3>
      <p>
        Built a cohesive Figma library and token architecture (colors, typography, spacing, elevation) from scratch. Standardizing patterns early allowed engineers to build complex interfaces 2x faster without waiting for mockups for every edge state.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Full-Stack Prototyping & Code</h3>
      <p>
        I bridged handoff friction by implementing production-ready HTML, CSS, and TypeScript directly when developers were constrained.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Autonomous Decision-Making</h3>
      <p>
        Partnered directly with founders and data scientists to shape the product roadmap. Made design calls autonomously, knowing when to ship rapid MVPs versus when to invest in deep polish.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>

<!-- 3. HOMEPAGE & CONVERSION EXPERIMENT -->
<section id="growth-experiment" class="cs-section">
  <h4 class="section-label">Homepage & Conversion</h4>
  <h2>Pivoting from generic value props to intent-driven conversion</h2>
  <p>
    Our top-of-funnel acquisition was leaking potential clients. The original homepage suffered from high bounce rates and unclear intent capture.
  </p>

  <div class="grid-2">
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>The Problem with V1</h3>
      <p>
        V1 led with the hypothesis that less and more. But, visitors didn’t immediately understand what specific problems coaching would solve or where to start.
      </p>
      <p style="color: #ef4444; font-weight: 500;">
        Friction: Users not understanding the product or service when landing on the page.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Hypothesis for V2</h3>
      <p>
        If give clients more information and explain the process and credibility more, they will be more clear on what coaching is and feel more confident in entering the conversion funnel.
      </p>
      <p style="color: #10b981; font-weight: 500;">
        Outcome: Direct clarity, lower barrier to starting assessment.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>

<!-- 4. CORE WORKFLOWS & AI SYSTEMS -->
<section id="platform-systems" class="cs-section">
  <h4 class="section-label">Core Workflows & AI</h4>
  <h2>Designing dual-sided experiences across clients and coaches</h2>
  <p>
    Managing a dual-sided marketplace means balancing two contrasting user types: clients who demand consumer simplicity, and coaches who need professional data density.
  </p>

  <h3>1. Building Trust in AI During Client Onboarding</h3>
  <p>
    Strawberry leverages AI models to make the coaching experience better and more personalized. However, coaching can be sensitive. Clients feared their sensitive vulnerabilities would be harvested or sold. I designed transparent onboarding and consent checkpoints explaining our de-identification, aggregation, and data privacy models upfront, boosting onboarding completion.
  </p>
  <div class="impact-holder size-a theme-strawberry">
    <p class="impact-label">Client onboarding: Transparent AI consent checkpoints that built trust</p>

  <h4 class="section-label">Role & Learnings</h4>
  <h2>Operating as the only designer and lessons from hyper-growth</h2>

  <div class="grid-2">
  <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Design as an Active Business Driver</h3>
      <p>
        Treating design as a growth engine and partnering with data science, running rigorous A/B tests, and seeing direct +$120K revenue spikes, reinforced that great product design is also about business outcomes, not just aesthetics.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Building Scalable Systems</h3>
      <p>
        Learned how to set up resilient foundations (tokens, patterns, and component libraries) without slowing down product delivery. Standardizing early was the a reason our lean team could ship so many features without accumulating massive design debt.
      </p>
