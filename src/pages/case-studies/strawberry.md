  </p>
  <p>
    As founding designer, I built the entire design system from scratch while designing and shipping 40+ major features across mobile and web platforms, contributing directly to $20M+ in revenue growth.
  </p>

  <div class="callout">
    <h3>Impact: Built the design system from 0→1, shipped 40+ major features, and drove conversion lifts of +20% (generating $120K in direct A/B tested revenue) supporting $20M+ ARR.</h3>
  </div>

  <div class="grid-2">
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Clients (Consumer Experience)</h3>
      <p>

      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Coaches (Professional Workstation)</h3>
      <p>

    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">

    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Full-Stack Prototyping</h3>

  </div>

  <div class="grid-2">
    <div class="grid-card">

<!-- 5. ROLE & LEARNINGS SECTION -->
<section id="reflection" class="cs-section">
  <h4 class="section-label">Role & Learnings</h4>
  <h2>Operating as sole designer and lessons from hyper-growth</h2>

  <div class="grid-2">
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Design System Architecture</h3>
      <p>
        Constructed a scalable component system from scratch in Figma. Established tokens for colors, typography, spacing, and elevation, collaborating with engineering to ensure 1:1 code parity.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Full-Stack Design Execution</h3>
      <p>
        Designed in Figma and implemented production code in HTML, CSS, and TypeScript. Reduced handoff friction and unblocked frontend developers on tight launch schedules.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Cross-Functional Leadership</h3>
      <p>
        Partnered with data scientists to identify drop-off zones, collaborated with founders on product roadmaps, and presented weekly design reviews to leadership.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Autonomous Decision-Making</h3>
      <p>
        Made design calls without requiring micro-approvals. Balanced speed with craft, knowing exactly when to ship rapid MVPs versus when to invest in deep polish.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <h3>Key Takeaways</h3>
  <div class="grid-3">
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Balancing Two User Archetypes</h3>
      <p>
        Designing for clients (who require simplicity) and coaches (who need data density) taught me to craft modular systems that excel at both extremes without compromise.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Building Scalable Systems Fast</h3>
      <p>
        Learned how to set up resilient foundations (tokens, patterns, and component libraries) while juggling multiple active sprint deadlines simultaneously.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid-card">
      <h3>Design as a Business Driver</h3>
      <p>
        Seeing direct revenue spikes from UX iterations reinforced that thoughtful design is a core business driver, not merely visual decoration.
      </p>
    </div>
</section>
